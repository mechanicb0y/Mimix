# Code of conduct

Mimix follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Voice work touches people's identities. Never use Mimix, its issues or its discussions to share someone's voice, or a clone of it, without their consent.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project's spaces is unacceptable, tell the maintainer privately. If you have no other way to reach them, open an issue that says only that you need to report a conduct problem, with no details, and you'll get a private contact.

Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may ban anyone from the project for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
