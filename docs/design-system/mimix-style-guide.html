Phase 1 · Brand and design system

Two voices in, one voice out.

Mimix tokens, scales and components, built on the Holst teal palette. Every colour pair on this page is checked against WCAG 2.2 AA in the browser as you read it. Switch themes at the top right.

1 · Palette

Seven colours from the photo

The Holst photo gives a single teal ramp from abyss to mist. Mimix uses it for every surface, text and brand role, and adds four new hues for status (success, warning, danger and a destructive fill) because the photo has no warm colours.

2 · WCAG 2.2 AA

Contrast table

Text needs 4.5:1 (SC 1.4.3). Input borders, focus rings and other UI parts need 3:1 (SC 1.4.11). The decorative --border is exempt. Light --accent is for icons and graphics only. Ratios below are recomputed live from the CSS on this page.

3c · Motion and layers

        
Three durations, two curves

        
150ms for hover and press, 250ms for menus and toasts, 400ms for modals and reveals. Enter with cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), exit with cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1). With reduced motion on, only opacity fades of 150ms or less remain, loops play once, and spinners become a still icon with “Loading…”.

4 · Icons

        
Lucide, 1.75px stroke

        
One open-source set, Lucide 1.57.0 (ISC licence), drawn at 16, 20 and 24px with round caps and joins. Icons take currentColor. A decorative icon gets aria-hidden="true"; an icon-only button gets an aria-label.

      

      

    


    
    

      

        
5 · Grid and media

        
Breakpoints, containers and media

        
Mobile first. Content sits in a 1200px container, full-bleed bands stop at 1440px. Gutters are 16px on phones, 24px on tablets and 32px on desktop. The grid is 4 columns under 768px, 8 to 1023px and 12 from 1024px, with 16, 24 and 24px gaps.

Brand story

        
Every voice is a mix. You sound a little like the people who raised you, the places you lived and the voices you loved listening to. Mimix is built on that idea. Pick two or three voices, blend them, and hear something new that still carries where it came from. Clone your own voice to read your scripts, design one from a few words, or turn a podcast into twelve languages over lunch. Every voice in Mimix comes with the speaker's consent and a record of how it was made, so creators, studios and teachers can use what they make with confidence.
